FAQ

Got Questions?

Everything you need before commissioning your first dental animation.

Ask on WhatsApp
01How long does a dental animation take?
+

Usually 2–4 business days depending on complexity. Simple explainer reels are often ready in 48 hours. Multi-scene 3D procedures may take up to 4 days.

02Do you write the dental script?
+

Yes — as a practicing dentist, I write every script to be clinically accurate and clear for patients. It's dentally precise, patient-friendly, and available in both Arabic and English at no extra cost.

03What if I need revisions?
+

Every project includes 2 free revision rounds. Because I understand dental context, most clients approve on first delivery with zero changes needed.

04Do you offer white-label for agencies?
+

Yes. I work as a silent production partner for dental marketing agencies worldwide — your brand, your client, I handle the animation behind the scenes.

05What format will I receive?
+

HD MP4 in 9:16 (Reels/Shorts) and 16:9 formats — optimized for Instagram, TikTok, clinic screens, and patient consultations, with and without voiceover on request.
